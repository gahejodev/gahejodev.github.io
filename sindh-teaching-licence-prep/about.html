About this preparation hub

The Sindh Teaching Licence Preparation Hub is an independent, free learning resource designed to help aspiring and serving teachers organise their study, revise education concepts and practise applying professional knowledge to classroom situations.

What learners can use

  • Topic-based preparation guidance and a structured study plan.
  • Practice questions with explanations.
  • Classroom case studies and constructed-response practice.
  • Extended-response prompts and suggested answer guidance.
  • Personal study notes and progress features saved locally in the learner's browser.

Editorial approach

Material is provided for learning and revision. Official syllabi, policies, examination notices and instructions may change. Learners should verify eligibility, test format, dates, fees and application requirements with STEDA and the relevant official testing agency before making decisions.

Suggested answers are learning guidance, not guaranteed examination answers. Learners should develop their own reasoning and use examples from real teaching practice.

Independence and accuracy

This website is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by STEDA, the Sindh Education & Literacy Department or any testing agency. Official sources take precedence over this study aid. If you notice an error or broken link, please send feedback through the project repository.
