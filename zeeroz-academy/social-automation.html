Facebook connection ready.

Facebook Reels upload is now supported: approve a post, attach a local video, then use “Upload Facebook Reel”. Instagram publishing is separate and currently requires a publicly accessible video URL, so local-only videos cannot be sent to Instagram without a hosting location.
