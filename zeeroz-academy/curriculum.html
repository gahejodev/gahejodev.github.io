Future-ready skills

Coding, Technology & AI Academy

Designed as a separate skill pathway that students can take alongside school or after completing their studies. No previous coding experience is required at the foundation level.

🧩

01 · Digital Foundations

Become a confident and safe digital learner.

  • Computer hardware, software and operating systems
  • Files, folders, cloud tools and productivity
  • Internet, networks and web fundamentals
  • Digital citizenship, privacy and cyber safety
🌐

02 · Web Development

Build and publish websites from scratch.

  • HTML structure and semantic web
  • CSS, responsive layouts and accessibility
  • JavaScript, DOM and interactive interfaces
  • Git, GitHub, hosting and portfolio websites
🐍

03 · Programming with Python

Learn to think like a programmer.

  • Variables, data types and operators
  • Conditions, loops, functions and modules
  • Lists, dictionaries, files and debugging
  • Algorithms, problem-solving and mini apps
📱

04 · Software & App Development

Turn ideas into useful digital products.

  • Programming fundamentals with C/C++
  • Databases, SQL and data modelling
  • APIs, JSON and application architecture
  • Android/app concepts and testing
🤖

05 · Artificial Intelligence

Understand AI, use it wisely and create with it.

  • AI concepts, machine learning and data
  • Generative AI and prompt design
  • AI-assisted research, coding and creativity
  • Bias, privacy, verification and responsible use
  • Introductory AI project and evaluation
📊

06 · Data & Analytics

Make sense of information and evidence.

  • Spreadsheets and data organisation
  • Charts, statistics and data storytelling
  • Python data tools at the advanced level
  • Introductory data science projects
🛡️

07 · Cybersecurity

Learn defensive security and safe computing.

  • Password safety and multi-factor security
  • Phishing, scams and social engineering
  • Networks and basic security concepts
  • Ethical, legal and defensive practice only
🎮

08 · Emerging Technology

Explore creative and hands-on technology.

  • Game design and interactive media
  • Robotics and physical computing concepts
  • Automation and no-code workflows
  • Internet of Things (IoT) foundations
🚀

09 · Digital Entrepreneurship

Learn how to take a digital idea forward.

  • Problem discovery and product thinking
  • UI/UX, prototyping and user testing
  • Teamwork, documentation and presentation
  • Portfolio, freelancing awareness and careers
Progression

Three levels. Real growth.

Students move forward when they demonstrate understanding, not just when they finish watching content.

Level 1 · Foundation

Understand

Build vocabulary, core concepts, digital confidence and safe habits. Use guided examples and short knowledge checks.

Level 2 · Builder

Apply

Solve problems, write and debug code, use tools thoughtfully, complete guided labs and explain decisions.

Level 3 · Creator

Create

Design original projects, test them, document the process, improve from feedback and present a portfolio.

Career & lifelong learning

Skills beyond the classroom

Job preparation and career learning will be connected with practical communication, digital literacy and problem-solving.

📝 Recruitment Test Preparation

General knowledge, English, mathematics, analytical reasoning, computer basics, subject knowledge and timed mock tests.

🎤 Communication & Interviews

Professional writing, speaking, interview practice, presentations, teamwork and workplace etiquette.

💼 Career Readiness

CV/resume preparation, digital portfolio, career exploration, learning plans and responsible online presence.

How every course will work

Learn → Practise → Prove → Improve

A consistent learning experience across academic subjects, job preparation and technology courses.

Concept lesson

Clear explanations, examples, diagrams and vocabulary.

Practice & feedback

Exercises, MCQs, short answers and guided coding tasks.

Assessment

Topic tests, mock exams, practical checks and rubrics.

Project & portfolio

Build, submit, explain and improve real work.

Quality promise: Official board requirements will be distinguished from Zeeroz Academy enrichment courses. We will not label an extra skill course as a board-mandated subject, and we will not claim that a lesson, certificate or course is available until it has actually been published and checked.
