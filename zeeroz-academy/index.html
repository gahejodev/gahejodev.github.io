Help centre

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about using Zeeroz Academy.

Is Zeeroz Academy free to use?

Public learning pages and the Sindh Teaching Licence preparation portal are available free. Any future paid product or service will be clearly identified before use.

Where can I prepare for the Sindh Teaching Licence test?

Open the dedicated preparation portal for practice MCQs, mock tests, CRQs, ERQs, case studies and study resources.

Are Class IX–XII resources available now?

Biology IX chapter packages are currently published and listed in the public course catalogue. Additional subjects and classes will appear here only after their content is prepared, checked and published.

Do I need to create an account?

You can browse the public catalogue without an account. A free, verified student account is required to open full lessons, quizzes, CRQs and ERQs in the Student Portal. The Study Desk tools on this homepage work without sign-in.

How can I suggest a topic or report an issue?

Email zeerozacademy@gmail.com with the page name and your suggestion or issue.
