Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how the independent Sindh Teaching Licence Preparation Hub handles information when you use this website.

Information you enter

The practice activities do not ask you to create an account or submit your name, phone number, school, examination number or other personal details. Do not type sensitive personal information into study notes or answer boxes.

Storage on your device

Some study features, such as preparation checklist progress, personal notes and quiz-attempt summaries, may be saved in your browser using local storage. This information is stored in the browser on the device you use and is not sent to this website's owner by those features. Anyone with access to the same browser or device may be able to view it. You can remove it by clearing this website's browser storage.

Cookies and advertising

This site is not currently presenting advertising through this study hub. If advertising services such as Google AdSense are added in the future, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure or personalise ads in accordance with their own policies and your settings. This policy will be updated before advertising is enabled.

External websites

Links to official departments and other external websites are provided for convenience. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies and practices. Review their policies before sharing information with them.

Children and learners

The learning material is intended for general exam preparation. Learners should avoid entering personal, confidential or identifying information into free-text fields, especially when using a shared device.

Changes and questions

This policy may be updated when site features change. For corrections or privacy questions, use the repository feedback page.

This is an independent educational resource and is not an official STEDA or Sindh Education & Literacy Department website.
