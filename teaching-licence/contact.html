Feedback and contact

Your feedback helps improve the learning material. You can report a broken link, factual correction, accessibility issue or suggestion through the project's public GitHub issue tracker.

Send feedback / report an issue ↗

Before sending feedback

  • Include the page or section name and describe the issue clearly.
  • For a factual correction, include a reliable official source where possible.
  • Do not post your CNIC, phone number, examination details, student information or other private data.

For official teaching licence eligibility, registration, examination dates or application questions, contact STEDA or the relevant official testing agency directly. This independent study hub cannot process applications or change official records.
